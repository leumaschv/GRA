Tuxedo Bar Cake

A tuxedo bar cake is a rich, multi-layered dessert featuring moist chocolate cake filled with alternating layers of dark chocolate mousse and vanilla cream cheese or white chocolate mousse, finished with a glossy chocolate ganache topping. If you've gone down the dessert aisle at warehouse grocery stores like Sam's Club or Costco, this type of cake should immediately be familiar. Though, making it at home should theoretically be much richer in taste with the recency of the ingredients as they cook and set sooner than when bought at the store.

Ingredients

For the Chocolate Cake Layers

  • 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup hot water

For the White Chocolate Mousse

  • 8 oz white chocolate, finely chopped
  • 1 ½ cups heavy cream (divided)
  • 1 tsp unflavored gelatin
  • 1 tbsp cold water

For the Chocolate Ganache

  • 8 oz dark or semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter (optional, used for a glossy finish)

Instructions

  1. Bake the chocolate cake layers at 350°F and allow them to cool completely.
  2. Prepare the white chocolate mousse and dark chocolate ganache layers.
  3. Assemble the chilled components into alternating layers.
  4. Refrigerate the final cake until it is fully set before slicing.
